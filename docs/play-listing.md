# Play Store listing copy

Paste into **Play Console → Grow → Store presence → Main store listing**.

Every claim here is checked against what the app actually does. Two things were
being oversold before this was written — a "global average" that did not exist,
and NG+ tracking that is free rather than paid — so treat this file as the
source of truth and change the app first if it stops matching.

Character limits are Play's own and are enforced by the form.

---

## App name

**Limit 30.** Currently 25.

```
Continue? — Death Counter
```

The bare brand would be `Continue?`, but that is close to meaningless in a
search result. The descriptor is what makes the icon legible to somebody
scrolling.

## Short description

**Limit 80.** Currently 76.

```
Count your deaths, run by run. See which games were really hardest for you.
```

Two sentences because the first alone reads like a novelty tally-counter, and
the ranking is the part that is actually unusual.

## Full description

**Limit 4000.** Currently ~2050.

Play strips most formatting, so this is plain text with blank lines and bullet
characters rather than markdown.

```
Every hard game has a number attached to it — the one you would quote if
somebody asked how that boss went. Continue? is where that number lives.

You die, you tap the button, the number goes up. That is genuinely the whole
interaction.


WHAT IT TRACKS

• Deaths per game and per run. NG, NG+ and NG+2 each keep their own count
  instead of blurring into one pile.

• Play time, on a session clock that keeps running while you are actually in
  the game — so deaths per hour means something.

• How long since your last death, for the moment you cannot remember whether
  you tapped or were just thinking about it.

• Per-game stats: today, this run, all time, and time played.


WHICH GAMES WERE ACTUALLY HARD

Continue? ranks your games by your own deaths per hour, and scores them by
where they sit against each other rather than by the raw rate. One brutal
half-hour cannot drag every other game down the scale.

It is your ranking, from your deaths. Not a review score, and not anybody
else's opinion.


YOUR TALLY STAYS YOURS

Your data lives on your phone and the app works completely offline. The cloud
backup exists for one reason: so you can get your tally back if you lose the
device.

• No ads. No analytics. No tracking. No account needed.
• An email address is optional, and only ever used to send a sign-in code so a
  backup can be recovered on a new phone.
• Earlier snapshots are kept, so a mistake is recoverable rather than final.
• Delete everything permanently, from inside the app, whenever you want.


FREE

Track up to three games, with everything above included.


ONE-TIME UNLOCK

Not a subscription. Buy it once.

• Unlimited games
• Your own lists — group games you think of as comparable, horror or
  souls-likes or whatever you like, and rank each list on its own terms
• Start tracking at any NG+ level, for a game you are already deep into
• Bring back games you swapped out, tallies intact


ANY GAME, REALLY

Built with souls-likes in mind, because that is where a death count gets worn
as a badge rather than hidden. But nothing here cares what you are playing.
Platformers, roguelikes, racing games, that one puzzle game that keeps getting
you — if it can kill you, it can be counted.


Game names and cover art provided by IGDB.
Made by Quiet Foundry.
```

## Deliberately not claimed

- **No comparison against other players.** The global average is not built.
- **NG+ tracking is not sold as a paid feature.** Advancing to NG+ and starting
  a fresh playthrough are both free; only jumping to an arbitrary cycle is not.
- **No price in the description.** Play localises it, and a hardcoded figure
  goes stale or reads as wrong in another currency.
- **No specific game titles.** Naming trademarked games in a listing invites a
  policy flag for implied association, and the genre words do the same job.
