Privacy Policy

Continue? — made by Quiet Foundry. Last updated 10 August 2026.

Continue? is a death counter for video games. It is built local-first: your tally lives on your phone, and the cloud copy exists only so you can get it back if you lose the device. There is no advertising, no analytics, no tracking, and nothing is sold or shared with anyone.

What is stored, and where

DataWhereWhy
The games you track, your death counts and their timestamps, play-time sessions, and run history Your phone. Also backed up to our database if backup is on. It is the app. Without it there is no tally.
An account identifier Our database Created automatically so a backup has an owner. It is anonymous unless you add an email.
Your email address (optional) Our database Only if you choose to add one. Used solely to send a six-digit sign-in code so you can recover your tally on a new phone. Never used for marketing.
App version and the time of the last backup Our database So a restored backup can be traced to the version that wrote it.
Suggestions you send (optional) Our database Only what you type, and only if you send one. Read by the developer to decide what to build. No reply is sent and no email address is collected with it.

There is no account and no password. You can use the entire app without ever entering an email address.

What is not collected

No advertising identifiers. No analytics or usage tracking. No location. No contacts. No device fingerprinting. No crash-reporting SDK. The app contains no third-party advertising or analytics code of any kind.

Notifications are generated on your own device — the reminder that your play-time tracker is still running. There is no push server and no device token is collected.

Who else is involved

ServiceWhat it sees
Supabase — database and sign-in codes, hosted in the EU (Ireland) Your backup, your account identifier, and your email address if you added one.
IGDB (Twitch) — game names and cover art Game searches are made by our server, not your phone, so IGDB does not see who searched. Cover images are loaded directly by your phone, so IGDB's image servers do receive your IP address, as with any image on the web.
Namecheap Private Email — outbound mail Your email address, when a sign-in code is sent to it.
Google Play Handles app distribution and, if you buy the one-time unlock, the payment. We never see your payment details.

How long it is kept

Your backup is kept for as long as you use the app. Alongside the current backup we retain earlier snapshots so a mistake is recoverable: everything from the last 48 hours, then one snapshot per day for 30 days. Older snapshots are deleted automatically.

If you delete your account, everything above is removed immediately and permanently — the current backup, every retained snapshot, and the account record itself. It cannot be recovered afterwards, including by us.

Your choices

  • Don't back up at all. The app works fully offline. Backup is a feature, not a requirement.
  • Don't add an email. Backup still works; it just cannot be recovered on a different phone.
  • Delete everything. In the app: Backup & restore → Delete my account and cloud data. Or use the data deletion request page if you no longer have the app installed.
  • Delete the local copy. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android settings, removes everything held on the phone.

If you are in the UK or EEA, you have the right to access, correct, export or erase your data, and to complain to your local data protection authority. In practice the app already gives you all of it directly: your data is on your own device, and deletion is one screen away. For anything else, email us.

Children

Continue? is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has provided us with an email address, contact us and we will delete it.
